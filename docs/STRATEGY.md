# Strategy Notes — the pivot, and what you're missing

You asked two real questions: *how do I build this* (answered in `BUILD_GUIDE.md`) and *am I missing anything* as you rename **Founders Decoded** → **"NO BS — Should You Buy This?"**. Here's the honest take.

## The pivot: keep the DNA, change the promise

Founders Decoded was about *origin stories*. "Should You Buy This?" is about a *decision*. That's a stronger promise — people actively search "should I buy X," so your titles map directly to intent — but don't throw away what made the old channel work. Your unfair advantage is combining both: **every review carries the founder/company story.** Most "should you buy" channels can't be bothered to explain who built the thing and why; you can, and this pipeline does it automatically (see the "Their founding story" section in every edition). That's your moat. Bridge the two audiences with one pinned video: *"Why Founders Decoded is becoming NO BS."*

On the name: **"NO BS" is a great brand, weak search term; "Should You Buy This?" is a weak brand, great search term.** Use both deliberately — **NO BS** as the channel identity/logo, **"Should you buy [X]?"** as the repeatable video-title format. Own the honesty positioning; let the format do the SEO.

## The one thing that makes or breaks this brand

Your entire value is *trust*. The moment a "don't buy / this is BS" verdict looks bought, the brand is dead. So two non-negotiables:
1. **A sponsorship never changes a verdict.** Sponsored placements must be disclosed *and* still honest. If you can't be honest about a sponsor's product, don't take the deal.
2. **Every factual claim links to a primary source** (the pipeline enforces this) and **AI output is human-checked before publish.** The approval email is your fact-check gate — actually read the sources, because AI can get a founding story subtly wrong.

## Gaps to close (the "am I missing anything" checklist)

- **Legal cover.** Add a persistent "opinion, not financial advice" line (done in the templates) and **FTC affiliate/sponsor disclosure**. When you say "skip this," stick to verifiable facts + clearly-framed opinion to stay clear of defamation.
- **Monetization path.** The "Who should buy this" section is a natural **affiliate-link** slot (Amazon/manufacturer programs). Later: sponsorships (disclosed), channel memberships, and a **paid newsletter tier** off the same daily edition. You already own the audience via the app + email list — that's the asset the algorithm can't take away.
- **The human layer the pipeline can't do.** It hands you research + a script skeleton. You still own the **3-second hook** (use the `🎬 THE NEWS` one-liner), **thumbnail**, **voice/face**, and **edit**. That's the actual product; the pipeline just removes the 2 hours of daily research.
- **Cadence realism.** Daily is brutal solo. Suggested rhythm: **1 daily Short** (hero story) + **1 weekly long-form** built from the week's roundup JSON. Same pipeline, two formats.
- **Copyright discipline.** Don't reuse others' footage/thumbnails. Use official press images, licensed b-roll, or your own. License your music.
- **Differentiation.** Your edge over the crowded "tech review" space: (a) **breadth** — AI + chips + robotics + eVTOL + drones in one place; (b) **founder-story depth**; (c) the **"is there a free/better alternative?"** angle, which is under-served and *extremely* shareable; (d) **speed** — automated same-day turnaround.
- **Feedback loop.** Track which categories/verdicts actually perform, and feed that back into `ranker.py` weights. Let the audience teach the algorithm what to surface.

## Suggested 3-week rollout

- **Week 1:** run the pipeline + approval email daily; start posting Shorts from the research (no app needed yet). Prove the content works.
- **Week 2:** deploy the web app (Vercel) + Supabase; start collecting email subscribers.
- **Week 3:** ship the Expo app to TestFlight/Play beta; turn on push. Now you own three distribution channels plus YouTube.

## Honest risks

- **Being wrong in public.** Mitigate with sources + the approval gate; correct fast and openly when you miss — that *builds* trust for a "NO BS" brand.
- **Burnout.** The automation is specifically to prevent this; resist the urge to also do the research manually.
- **Over-reliance on one platform.** Exactly why the app + newsletter matter — build the list from day one.

Bottom line: the system you now have removes the single biggest bottleneck (daily research + a consistent, sourced script). What's left is the part only you can do — show up on camera with a point of view and stay honest. That's the whole brand.

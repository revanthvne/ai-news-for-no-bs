The story

What makes this unusual is that node prices normally drift down as yields mature; instead TSMC is raising them because 3nm utilization is estimated above 120% this year and above 110% in 2027. Their founding story
TSMC (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company) was founded in 1987 in Hsinchu, Taiwan by Morris Chang, backed by the Taiwanese government and Philips. Who should USE this
Fabless chip designers (Nvidia, AMD, Apple, Qualcomm, Broadcom, MediaTek) who need leading-edge 3nm/5nm capacity; hyperscalers designing custom AI accelerators; equity investors and supply-chain planners tracking cost pass-through into GPUs and phones.
Who should PURCHASE this
This is a capacity-allocation and equity story, not a consumer purchase. Enterprises that must 'buy' are the fabless designers locking in wafer supply agreements — and they have essentially no alternative at 3nm. Investors weighing TSMC stock (NYSE: TSM) get a company with structural pricing power but heavy Taiwan geopolitical and capex risk. What makes it special: no one else can volume-produce sub-3nm AI chips. Who should NOT: anyone expecting these price hikes to reverse soon, or betting on a near-term China/Taiwan de-risking.
Free / better alternatives
There is no 'free' alternative at the leading edge.

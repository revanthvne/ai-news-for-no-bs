Star counts put it well into the 110k-119k range by mid-2026 (Wikipedia recorded 89.2k in September 2025). In April 2026, TechCrunch reported ComfyUI's company, Comfy Org, raised $30M at a roughly $500M valuation led by Craft Ventures, as creators sought more control over AI-generated media. The project actively ships a desktop app and has added support for newer open video models such as WAN 2.1 and HunyuanVideo (including image-to-video), extending it from images into video pipelines.
Their founding story
ComfyUI was created by the pseudonymous developer 'comfyanonymous,' with its first release in January 2023, aiming to improve on existing Stable Diffusion UIs with a flexible node-based design. comfyanonymous worked at Stability AI until mid-2024; after leaving, the core developers formed the Comfy Org organization (June 2024) to steward the project and build a company around it, later raising venture funding.
Who should USE this
Artists, technical creators and researchers who want fine-grained, reproducible control over AI image and video generation and are willing to learn a node-based workflow.
Who should PURCHASE this
The software is free and open source; costs are optional - your own GPU (or a rented cloud GPU) to run heavier image and video models locally.
Free / better alternatives
ComfyUI is the free/open alternative to paid generative tools like Midjourney, DALL-E and Runway; other free options include Automatic1111 and Fooocus for simpler image generation.
